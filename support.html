Support

How do I get started with the app? ▶
Getting started is easy! Simply download the app from the App Store and open it. You'll be guided through a quick setup process to help you get started. If you need help with any specific features, feel free to reach out to our support team.
How can I restore my purchase? ▶
To restore your purchase, make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID that you used for the original purchase. Then, navigate to the app's purchase screen and tap on "Restore" or "Restore Purchases". The app will verify your purchase and restore access to premium features.
I'm experiencing an issue. What should I do? ▶
If you're experiencing any issues with the app, please try restarting the app first. If the problem persists, try restarting your device. For more specific issues or if you need additional help, please contact our support team using the button below. We're here to help!
